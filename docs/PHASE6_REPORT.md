# Sportozen Academy — Phase 6 final QA and release-readiness report

Written for: the repository owner, who will decide on acceptance testing and on the deployment.

Phase 5 and Phase 6 are reported separately. Phase 5 is in `PHASE5_REPORT.md` and is frozen (`phase5-final-snapshot`, `phase5-*.patch`). This report covers Phase 6 only, plus the final Phase 5 verification that the brief asked for first.

Nothing was committed, pushed, merged, deployed, reset, stashed or cleaned. The main checkout, Phase 2 worktrees, clean validation copy, recovery folders and original databases were not touched; every test used throw-away copies or new databases under `.local/`. No email was sent, no DNS, account or credential was created, no payment was processed, no paid service was bought.

## 1. Executive summary

**Recommendation: ready for the owner's acceptance testing on a local or staging build; not yet ready for production, and not production validated.** The remaining gaps are external (a server, a domain, an email provider, alert routing, a real device test) or product decisions (section 12), none of them a defect in the code that was tested.

- Phase 5 final verification passed (section 2). One test assertion needed correcting after the font change; no product defect.
- Phase 6 found and fixed **four defects** that earlier phases had missed (section 11): credit amounts lost their minus sign in invoice PDFs, the parent Fees screen was visibly broken on tablet and desktop widths, a set of WCAG failures, and long unbroken text overflowing some cards. No Critical or High defect is open.
- **No security failure was found.** The new black-box suite attacked two academies with wrong credentials, wrong academy, tampered identifiers, forged origins and anonymous requests (section 5): everything was refused, and the victims' data was read back unchanged.
- **Money agrees everywhere:** owner API, guardian API, stored rows, and the PDF text, for partial, full, duplicate, rejected, credited, waived and odd-cent cases (section 6).
- **Final regression on the final tree (third full run):** typecheck, lint and all builds pass; 1247 passed | 4 skipped (1251) in unit tests; 74 main browser tests, 2 account-security, 6 email-auth, 12 report-review; WSL release, rollback, ops, host and Docker nginx tests pass (section 10).
- **Performance** reproduces: mobile median load 5.30 s → 2.66 s, transfer 965 → 462 KB, first paint unchanged at 772 ms (section 9).
- **Not done, said plainly:** no formal penetration test; no real device or network; Firefox and WebKit were exercised by the full page sweep, not by the whole end-to-end suite; WhatsApp and live email are not tested; the ZeptoMail adapter is documented but not built; the platform has no academy approval step (a product gap).

## 5. Multi-tenancy and security results

`tests/e2e/phase6-isolation.spec.ts` (real server, real sessions, two academies; all assertions passed):

1. **Another academy's owner and a stranger:** 16 read endpoints each (workspace, bootstrap, home, records, a record by id, finance, an invoice PDF, communication, staff announcements, guardian announcements, a private-message read, trials, training) all 403; five write endpoints (create player, invoice, invite, announcement, batch) and a configuration PATCH all 403; the victim academy's players, invoice amounts and paid totals were read back unchanged.
2. **Identifiers from academy B used inside academy A's own path:** reading, renaming, paying an invoice of, downloading the PDF of, and announcing to a batch of the other academy all refused; nothing changed.
3. **Coaches:** each sees only the players of their own batches; cannot read or edit another coach's player; cannot announce to another batch or to a mixed set; cannot read finance, create invoices, invite, rename the academy or approve an announcement even holding the current version and a valid preview.
4. **Parents:** see only their own child; another child's record, finance query, invoice PDF and receipt PDF refused; cannot invoice, record a payment against their own invoice, create records, invite, announce, create batches, read the staff announcement route or patch the academy.
5. **Private messages:** a parent cannot read, reply into, or open a conversation about another family's child, nor as another guardian; a parent of academy B cannot reach academy A's conversations by either academy path; injected text never appears.
6. **Announcements:** only the intended guardian sees an approved announcement; the other family in the same academy and a family in another academy see nothing; staff use only the staff route.
7. **Platform administrator:** non-administrators get 403 on list and change; an administrator sees all academies but has no academy membership and gets 403 on academy data; pausing an academy closes it for its owner, coach and parent and not for another academy; forged origin 403; bad status 400; unknown academy 404.
8. **Anonymous and forged origin:** every route 401 anonymously; forged or `null` origin 403 on writes with no record created.
9. **Invitation tokens:** a token cannot be accepted by another account; garbage tokens refused.
10. **Errors:** no SQL, file paths or stack traces; `no-store` on refusals.

Also verified in earlier runs of this phase: strict CSP (nonce and `strict-dynamic`, `font-src 'self'`) with no violations, console errors or failed requests on 16 pages; no HTML-injection sinks in the source; data-integrity checker clean on three databases; seven older databases migrated through migration 011 without losing a table or changing a row, repeatable, originals untouched (`evidence/p6-migration-report.json`).

Authentication, sessions, password reset, MFA, CSRF and origin checks, rate limits, logging hygiene and file upload validation are covered by the existing unit and browser suites that passed in the final run (`auth-origin`, `auth-lifecycle`, `email-auth` 6 tests, `account-security` 2 tests, `rate-limit-*`, `log-hygiene`, `png-logo`). One observation, not a vulnerability: on an announcement `approve` command a stale version number is answered with 409 before the role is checked (403); the role check still applies once the version is right (L7).

**No formal penetration test was performed.** These are application-level checks by the author of the tests.

## 6. Financial workflow verification

`tests/e2e/phase6-finance.spec.ts` recomputes every figure independently from the stored invoice, adjustment and payment rows and compares it with the owner's API, each guardian's API and the generated PDFs.

| Case                          | Verified                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fee plan                      | saved; amount 0, negative, fractional, free-with-amount, unknown frequency, custom-without-days all refused (400); currency cannot change                             |
| Plan to invoice               | plan, batch assignment, a monthly suggestion of exactly ₹3,333.33                                                                                                     |
| Partial payment               | ₹12,345.67 invoice, ₹5,000 verified: balance ₹7,345.67, status PARTIAL; a pending reference does not reduce the balance                                               |
| Duplicates                    | the same reference typed in three ways is recorded once (409); a retry of the same request returns the same payment; a reused key with other content is refused (409) |
| Rejected payment              | never counted                                                                                                                                                         |
| Overpayment and paid invoices | refused; a paid invoice takes nothing more; a decided payment is final                                                                                                |
| Discount                      | credit above the balance refused; a ₹500 credit leaves ₹2,000 billed and payable                                                                                      |
| Waiver                        | full credit gives WAIVED, not payable                                                                                                                                 |
| Odd cents                     | ₹999.99 plus a 1-minor debit; three payments of 333.33 leave exactly 1 minor; the last payment closes it as PAID                                                      |
| Zero-charge                   | requires an explicit reason and confirmation                                                                                                                          |
| Dates and status              | overdue versus pending by due date; invalid charge, currency, period and date refused                                                                                 |
| Totals                        | invoice count, billed, collected and outstanding in the summary equal the independent sum; collected = ₹12,345.67 + ₹2,000 + ₹999.99 + 0.01                           |
| Receipts                      | exactly one per verified payment                                                                                                                                      |
| Guardians                     | see identical numbers for their own child's invoices and nothing else                                                                                                 |
| Documents                     | every invoice and receipt is a valid PDF; a parent downloads only their own                                                                                           |

**PDF text, extracted and compared** (outside the repository, `evidence/p6-pdf/`): for 14 PDFs from the seeded academy every billed, paid and outstanding figure matched the API; for an odd-amount invoice the PDF printed original ₹12,345.67, adjustment ₹345.67, billed ₹12,000.00, paid ₹5,000.01, outstanding ₹6,999.99. **That check found defect D1**: the adjustment lost its minus sign. Fixed and re-extracted: `Approved adjustments: -₹345.67`.

The UI (Fees screen) shows the same numbers: ₹5,000.00 collected, ₹7,345.67 outstanding, ₹12,345.67 billed, 41% collection rate (500,000 / 1,234,567 = 40.5%, rounded), for owner and parent. Currency formatting and parsing for INR, JPY (no decimals) and KWD (three decimals) are covered by the existing `finance-money` tests. Billing-period arithmetic for every frequency is covered by the existing `finance` and `finance-rules` tests (79 finance unit tests ran and passed); the system test exercises the monthly case end to end.

Not tested: a live payment gateway (none is connected, by design); tax; multi-currency within one academy beyond what the unit tests cover.
